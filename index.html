Creative Creator Program

How to publish your Creative map

Project Neo brings back classic Fortnite seasons through one launcher. Choose a season, install what you need, and jump back into the old days.

If you want to be part of our Creative Creators team and start publishing your map creations to the public with us planning in future to extend this program with even more exclusive rewards for you which depends on your performance and results over time, here is

How To Become A Creative Creator:

- You need to be eligible to be a content creator for Neo by respecting the content creator rules. If you are already a content creator for Neo, you are automatically in the Creative Creator program. If you consider yourself eligible for this role, you can read more about the content creator here.

- If you are a Crew or Crew+ member, you will automatically have the permission publishing your maps to the public, but you will not receive any bonus rewards for people playing your map. The only rewards you will receive are the ones that the subscriptions come with.

24/7 public Creative lobbies24/7 public Creative lobbies

We are hosting Creative servers 24/7 and hundreds of maps have already been published by our creators available to play for everyone, for free.

In the Hub, you can go to the Featured Islands rifts, select a rift and enter a map code of your choice, based on what map you want to play. To explore all the available maps, scroll up to the top of this page or use the search box from the top right on this page.

Creative lobbies can go up to 32 concurent players each, so you will always find someone to play with. In our discord, you can also find people to play with in #looking-for-group.
